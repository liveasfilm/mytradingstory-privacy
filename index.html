1. 개요

본 개인정보처리방침(이하 "본 방침")은 모바일 게임 My Trading Story(이하 "본 앱")가 이용자의 개인정보를 어떻게 수집·이용·보관·파기하는지를 설명합니다. 본 앱은 개인 개발자 liveasfilm(이하 "운영자")이 개발 및 운영하며, 운영자는 「개인정보 보호법」(대한민국), GDPR, CCPA 등 관련 법규를 준수합니다.

2. 수집하는 정보

2.1 자동으로 수집되는 정보

  • 익명 인증 식별자: Firebase Authentication이 발급하는 익명 UID. 임의의 문자열이며 그 자체로는 개인을 식별할 수 없습니다.
  • 광고 송출용 식별자: Google AdMob SDK가 광고 송출을 위해 처리하는 식별자(예: Android 광고 ID, Apple IDFA). 본 앱은 이를 직접 저장·전송하지 않으며, AdMob의 정책에 따라 처리됩니다.

2.2 이용자가 직접 입력하는 정보

  • 닉네임(최대 24자): 리더보드(순위표)에 점수를 등록할 때 입력합니다. 실명·연락처 등 개인정보 입력은 권장하지 않습니다.

2.3 서버(Firestore)에 저장되는 정보 — 리더보드 등록 시에만

  • 익명 UID 또는 플랫폼 플레이어 ID
  • 닉네임(이용자가 입력한 표시명)
  • 최종 총자산(KRW), 게임 달성 시각, 게임 플레이 시각
  • 인증 제공자 종류(anonymous, gamecenter, playgames)
  • 앱 버전 및 서버 측 업데이트 시각

2.4 단말기에만 저장되는 정보(서버로 전송되지 않음)

  • 게임 진행 상태(보유 자산, 거래 내역, 월별 스냅샷, 캐시·포지션 등)
  • 최근 게임 기록(최대 50개)
  • BGM/효과음 음소거 설정, 프리미엄(광고 제거) 활성 여부, 실명 표기 옵션
  • 익명 플레이어 UUID(SharedPreferences 보관)
  • SQLite 데이터베이스(game_data.db)에 포함된 시세·거시지표 등 게임 데이터

2.5 인앱결제(IAP) 정보

  • 제공 상품: remove_ads(광고 제거), real_names(실제 종목명 표시).
  • 결제·영수증은 Apple App Store / Google Play가 처리하며, 운영자는 결제 카드 번호 등 결제 수단 정보를 수집·보관하지 않습니다. 본 앱은 활성/비활성 상태만 단말기에 저장합니다.

2.6 선택적 플랫폼 로그인 정보

  • 이용자가 Apple Game Center 또는 Google Play Games에 로그인한 경우, 해당 플랫폼이 제공하는 플레이어 ID·표시명을 리더보드에 사용합니다. 로그인하지 않으면 익명 UUID가 사용됩니다.

2.7 수집하지 않는 정보

  • 위치 정보, 연락처, 사진, 카메라, 마이크
  • 분석(Analytics) SDK 데이터, 크래시 리포트, 푸시 알림용 토큰
  • 이메일·전화번호·실명 등 별도의 회원 정보(본 앱은 회원가입 절차가 없습니다)

3. 수집·이용 목적

  • 게임 기능 제공 및 진행 상태 저장
  • 리더보드 순위 표시 및 운영
  • 광고 송출(프리미엄 미구매 이용자 한정)

  • 법령상 의무 이행 및 분쟁 대응

6. 광고 및 트래킹

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") describes how the mobile game My Trading Story (Korean title: 껄무새와 함께하는 타임슬립 부자 게임; the "App") collects, uses, retains, and deletes user information. The App is developed and operated by an individual developer, liveasfilm (the "Operator"), who endeavors to comply with applicable laws including the Personal Information Protection Act (Republic of Korea), GDPR, and CCPA.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Automatically Collected

  • Anonymous authentication ID: An anonymous UID issued by Firebase Authentication. It is a random string and does not by itself identify an individual.
  • Advertising identifiers: Identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID, Apple IDFA) processed by the Google AdMob SDK to serve ads. The App does not directly store or transmit these; they are handled per AdMob's policies.

2.2 Information You Provide

  • Display name (max 24 characters): Submitted when you register a score on the leaderboard. We discourage entering real names, contact details, or other PII.

2.3 Stored on Server (Firestore) — Only When You Submit a Leaderboard Entry

  • Anonymous UID or platform player ID
  • Display name you entered
  • Final total assets (KRW), achievement timestamp, gameplay timestamp
  • Authentication provider type (anonymous, gamecenter, playgames)
  • App version and server-side update timestamp

2.4 Stored Only on Your Device (Not Transmitted)

  • Game progress (holdings, trade history, monthly snapshots, cash, positions, etc.)
  • Recent game records (up to 50 entries)
  • BGM/SFX mute state, premium (ad-removed) status, real-names display option
  • Anonymous player UUID stored in SharedPreferences
  • SQLite database (game_data.db) containing in-game market/macro data

2.5 In-App Purchases (IAP)

  • Available products: remove_ads and real_names.
  • Payments and receipts are handled by Apple App Store / Google Play. The Operator does not collect or retain payment instrument details. The App stores only an active/inactive flag locally on your device.

2.6 Optional Platform Sign-In

  • If you sign in to Apple Game Center or Google Play Games, the App uses the player ID and display name provided by the platform for the leaderboard. If you do not sign in, an anonymous UUID is used instead.

2.7 Information We Do Not Collect

  • Location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone
  • Analytics SDK data, crash reports, push-notification tokens
  • Email, phone number, real name, or any account-registration data (the App has no sign-up flow)

3. Purposes of Collection and Use

  • Providing the game and saving game progress
  • Operating and displaying the leaderboard
  • Serving ads (only to non-premium users)
  • Processing in-app purchases and preventing abuse
  • Complying with legal obligations and handling disputes

4. Retention and Deletion

  • Local data: Removed when you uninstall the App.
  • Leaderboard data (Firestore): Deleted promptly upon your request. In the absence of a request, retained to operate the service.
  • Anonymous UIDs do not in themselves contain personally identifiable information.

5. Third-Party Services and Data Processors

The App uses the following third-party services. Their respective privacy policies apply to their data processing.

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore) — Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob — AdMob data handling
  • Apple Game Center — Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Games Services — Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store / Google Play (in-app purchases) — respective policies of Apple and Google

6. Advertising and Tracking

  • The App does not use any first-party analytics or behavior-tracking SDK.
  • Banner ads are served via Google AdMob and are not displayed once the premium (remove-ads) IAP is active.
  • The App does not request iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT) authorization and therefore does not perform IDFA-based personalized tracking.
  • If we introduce analytics or expand data processing in the future, this Policy will be updated in advance.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 14 (or under 13 under the U.S. COPPA standard) and does not knowingly collect personal information from such children. If a parent or guardian becomes aware that a child's information has been registered with the App, please contact us at the address below and we will delete it promptly.

8. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information and may exercise them through the contact below.

  • Access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, and portability
  • Deletion of your leaderboard entry
  • Withdrawal of consent (e.g., opting out of advertising in future versions)

9. Data Security

  • Server communications are encrypted in transit using HTTPS (TLS).
  • Access to Firestore is controlled by security rules defined in firestore.rules.
  • No transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be perfectly secure; the Operator applies reasonable safeguards.
